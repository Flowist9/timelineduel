# TimelineDuel

**TimelineDuel is a Flutter-based history game that turns historical knowledge into competitive, interactive gameplay.**

Players collect historical figures, build their knowledge through timeline-based challenges, compete against others, and progress through a game system combining history, strategy, and trivia.

> **Project Status:** Active Development

---

## About the Game

TimelineDuel is designed around a simple idea: learning history becomes much more engaging when historical events and people are placed in relation to one another rather than learned as isolated facts.

The app combines timeline challenges with collectible historical figures and competitive game mechanics. Players can test their chronological knowledge, build their collection, complete challenges, earn rewards, and compete with other players.

The project is being developed as a full mobile application rather than a standalone prototype, with progression, social features, online gameplay, and persistent user data forming part of the wider game experience.

---

## Features

### Timeline Gameplay

Place historical events and figures in the correct chronological order and test your understanding of how history fits together.

### Battles

Compete through history-based challenges and use your knowledge to progress through battles.

### Online Battles

Challenge other players and bring the core TimelineDuel gameplay into a competitive multiplayer environment.

### Historical Figures

Discover and collect historical figures from different periods and areas of history.

### Collection System

Build a personal collection as you play and unlock additional historical content.

### Progression

Earn progress through gameplay and develop your account over time rather than playing isolated rounds.

### Achievements

Complete milestones and challenges to unlock achievements.

### Daily Challenges

Return for new challenges and opportunities to earn progress and rewards.

### Leaderboards

Compare your performance with other players.

### Friends

Connect with other players and access the social side of TimelineDuel.

### Shop

An integrated reward and shop system connects gameplay progression with unlockable content.

## Core Systems

TimelineDuel is built around several interconnected systems rather than a single game screen:

**Gameplay → Rewards → Collection → Progression → Competition**

This allows individual history challenges to contribute to a persistent player experience.

The project includes systems for:

- Historical content and character data
- Timeline-based game logic
- Battles and online battles
- Player progression
- Collections
- Achievements
- Daily challenges
- Friends and social functionality
- Leaderboards
- Rewards and shop functionality
- Persistent Firebase-backed data

## Motivation

History is often presented as a collection of individual dates, names, and events. TimelineDuel explores a different approach: understanding history through **chronology, relationships, and comparison**.

The goal is to create a game that can be played for entertainment while naturally encouraging players to develop a better sense of when historical people and events actually existed in relation to one another.
